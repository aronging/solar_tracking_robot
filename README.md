# solar_tracking_robot
MXET 345 Lab Final Project

Two-axis solar tracker on the MXET 345 lab robot: a pan-tilt panel mount driven by the robot's Raspberry Pi.

- `Code/`: tracker software (Python, Raspberry Pi). Start with [`Code/README.md`](Code/README.md).
- `CAD/`: current STEP parts and `Solar Tracker Assembly.step` (Nov 17); `Purchased Parts/`, `Test Prints/` and `_superseded/` (old revisions).
- [`Design Overview.md`](Design%20Overview.md): what the system does, the algorithm, conventions, numerical methods, test plan and open items.
- [`Hardware & BOM.md`](Hardware%20%26%20BOM.md): wiring, I2C map, parts and costs.
- [`Course Requirements.md`](Course%20Requirements.md): grading, deliverables, team roles and the lab robot.
- `Hardware List & Assembly Guide.pdf`: mechanical assembly reference (re-exported Nov 17 from the live guide).
- `Solar Tracker – Numerical Methods Summary.docx`: one-page list of the numerical methods.
